Truth & Dare offers interactive party gameplay, including:

  • Truth or Dare Gameplay: Hundreds of curated challenges across multiple age-appropriate categories (Kids, Teens, Adults, Couples/Spicy, Extreme, and Custom).

3. Information We Collect and Store

A. Local On-Device Data (Not Transmitted to Developers)

All gameplay content created or managed by players is stored strictly locally on your device using Android local storage (Room Database and Jetpack DataStore):

  • Player profiles and names chosen during the setup screen.
  • Scores, round progress, and penalty counts.
  • Custom questions entered through the Custom Question editor.
  • App settings and audio preferences (sound effects, music volume, and vibration toggles).

This local data never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers.

B. Personal Data We Do NOT Collect

To ensure complete user privacy, Truth & Dare does not collect or request:

  • Your real name, postal address, or telephone number.
  • Email address or social media login credentials.
  • GPS or precise device geolocation.
  • Contacts, photos, media files, or microphone/camera recordings.
  • Financial or credit card details.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Truth & Dare free to download and play, the Application integrates Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display ads (including optional Rewarded Video Ads).

What AdMob May Collect Automatically

When you connect to the internet, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process specific device and interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID), device model, operating system version, and screen resolution.
  • Network Information: General IP address (used for approximate country/city level geolocation) and network connection type.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad impressions, video playback completion, and clicks.
  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Crash reports and SDK telemetry to monitor ad rendering performance.

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You can reset your device's Advertising ID or opt out of interest-based ads at any time through your Android device settings:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Reset advertising ID or toggle on Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For further details regarding Google's ad privacy practices, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Policies.
